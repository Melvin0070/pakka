A voice agent acts on what it heard. Pramaan checks it first.

A speech-to-speech model goes from sound straight to action. When it logs an amount, a date or a phone number, nothing in between checks the value. Pramaan holds that action for up to 1.2 seconds, compares it with independent recognisers, and then lets it through, corrects it, or asks one question.
